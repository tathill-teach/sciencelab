DAY 1 • LIGHT DETECTIVES

☀️ Light Energy

Let’s investigate what makes light and what we can see because of light!

🎮 Play “Let’s Sort!”📽️ Open Original Day 1 Slides (PowerPoint)

🎯 Learning Target

I can: identify objects that produce their own light and objects that do not.

Success check: I can sort examples into “Light Source” and “Not a Light Source” and explain that the Moon reflects sunlight.

🗣️ Vocabulary

lightlight sourcereflectenergy

Light source: something that gives off its own light.

Reflect: when light bounces off a surface.

🧑‍🏫 Day 1 Lesson Flow

  1. Engage: Ask, “What helps us see when a room is dark?”
  2. Teach: Compare the Sun, a lamp that is on, and the Moon.
  3. Investigate: Use a flashlight and cardboard box to notice how light travels through an opening.
  4. Practice: Play the “Let’s Sort!” game together.
  5. Exit ticket: Name one light source and one object that is not a light source.

📦 Materials & Safety

  • Flashlight with working batteries
  • Cardboard box with a small opening prepared by an adult
  • Picture cards or sorting game on student iPads
  • Never shine a flashlight into anyone’s eyes.
  • Use only a battery-powered candle for the picture example; no open flame is needed.

🔎 Box Investigation

  1. Place a small object inside the box.
  2. Close the box and look through the opening. What can you see?
  3. Shine the flashlight through the opening toward the object.
  4. Describe what changed when light reached the object.

Ask: “What helped you see the object?”

✅ Quick Check

Ask students to respond orally, point, or show fingers:

  1. Does a switched-off flashlight make light?
  2. Is the Moon a light source?
  3. What does a mirror do with light?

Look for: students distinguish objects that give off light from objects that reflect light.
